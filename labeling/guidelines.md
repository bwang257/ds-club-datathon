# Category labeling guidelines

Label each product with **what it is**, not which device it's used with.
Pick exactly one of the categories below.

| Category | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Audio | headphones, earbuds (and their cases), speakers, soundbars, microphones and mic stands, amplifiers, subwoofers, turntables, radios, two-way radios / walkie-talkies, CB radios, voice recorders, DACs, instrument cables |
| Bags & Cases | laptop bags, backpacks, sleeves and briefcases *made for a device*, camera bags, hard protective cases (Pelican, Nanuk), tech/cable organizer pouches |
| Batteries | AA/AAA/coin/button/lantern batteries, rechargeable batteries, chargers for loose or removable batteries (incl. camera battery packs) |
| Cables & Charging | cables, chargers and charging stations, power adapters, power banks, USB hubs and dongles, cable management |
| Cameras & Photography | cameras (incl. security cameras, action cams, instant cameras, drones), lenses, tripods, gimbals, flashes, studio lights, camera mounts, photo film, capture cards, video production / conferencing gear (ATEM switchers, SDI/HDMI converters, Rally Bar) |
| Car Electronics | anything sold for use in a car: radar detectors, dash cams, car stereos, car amps and speakers, car chargers, FM transmitters, install kits, wiring harnesses, car antennas; also radio / police scanners |
| Computers & Laptops | laptops, desktops, mini PCs, CPUs, GPUs, RAM, motherboards, PC cases, PC power supplies, fans/coolers, thermal paste, expansion cards, laptop docking stations, dev boards (Arduino, Jetson), laptop accessories (stands, privacy screens, Surface covers) |
| Gaming | consoles, controllers, VR headsets, gaming wheels/sticks/cockpits, amiibo, video games |
| Keyboards & Mice | keyboards, mice, trackballs, trackpads, mouse pads, wrist rests, keyboard/mouse combos, stream decks |
| Marine & GPS | fish finders, transducers, chartplotters, marine radios, satellite communicators (inReach) |
| Monitors & Displays | computer monitors, portable monitors, commercial displays, monitor accessories (arms, stands, risers, privacy filters, light bars) |
| Networking | routers, mesh WiFi, modems, switches, access points, WiFi extenders, powerline adapters, ethernet cables/couplers, PoE injectors, network adapters |
| Office & School Supplies | office *devices* and their supplies: labels and label makers, label tapes, calculators (incl. printing calculators), shredders, laminators and laminating pouches, binding machines and combs, barcode scanners, presenters, printable paper/cards |
| Optics | binoculars, telescopes, microscopes, monoculars, rangefinders, spotting scopes |
| Other | electronics that genuinely fit none of the above |
| Phone & Tablet Accessories | accessories for phones/tablets: cases, screen protectors, phone mounts/stands/grips, styluses, trackers (AirTag) |
| Phones & Tablets | the devices themselves: smartphones, tablets, e-readers (Kindle), home/cordless/corded/VoIP/desk phones (incl. amplified senior phones) |
| Power Strips & Surge Protectors | power strips, surge protectors, UPS battery backups, extension cords, outlets, smart plugs, power conditioners, PDUs, timers, wall plates |
| Printers & Ink | printers, scanners (document/photo), ink, toner, printheads, photo and printer paper |
| Smart Home & Wearables | smart speakers/displays, digital picture frames, doorbells, home-security sensors, smart locks, smartwatches, fitness trackers, watch bands, smart glasses |
| Storage & Memory Cards | SD/microSD/CompactFlash/CFexpress cards, blank camcorder tapes (MiniDV, Hi8), card readers, flash drives, hard drives and SSDs (internal and external), drive docks / duplicators / enclosures, NAS, recordable CDs/DVDs, optical drives |
| TV & Home Entertainment | TVs, projectors, projector screens, streaming devices (Roku, Fire TV), Blu-ray/DVD players, TV remotes, TV mounts, antennas |
| **Non-Electronics** | not consumer electronics at all: generic bags with no device, toys, personal care, kitchen, pet, furniture, hand tools, apparel, cleaning products, household fans (bathroom exhaust), plain stationery (binders, folders, pens, pencils, markers, sticky notes, staplers, whiteboards, envelopes). These get dropped. |

Tie-breakers:
- **Bundles:** label the main product ("Camera Bundle with Bag" -> Cameras).
- **Accessories:** accessories for phones/tablets -> Phone & Tablet
  Accessories. Other accessories go with what the accessory *is* (a
  laptop sleeve -> Bags & Cases, a laptop charger -> Cables & Charging, a
  monitor arm -> Monitors & Displays, a TV mount -> TV & Home Entertainment).
- **Truly ambiguous:** pick the best fit and mark it unsure / add a note.
- **Docks:** laptop docking station (monitor outputs) -> Computers; small USB
  hub -> Cables & Charging; phone/watch charging dock -> Cables & Charging;
  controller / Switch dock -> Gaming; hard-drive dock -> Storage.
- **Cables** go in Cables & Charging whatever they connect (HDMI, PC power
  cables), except instrument cables (Audio) and car chargers (Car).
